A starting point

You're already paying a film crew for every listing, and the footage could be doing a lot more than one YouTube video. The two reels above are simple edits from the YouTube uploads. They can be refined around what Mapro wants to say and the image you want the brand to have, and with the source files, and the chance to film with your agents, we could take them to the next level. These two are short, but longer pieces matter too.

How we'd work

For each new listing, all I need from your side is the details, the photos, and the film if it's ready. I'll come back with storyboards like the ones below, with the one I'd recommend at the top and the others as alternatives. You pick one, change anything you like, and I make it. You can change things along the way too, and if marketing or legal need to sign it off first, that's built in. I'd much rather shape the ideas with you than hand over a video every week and hope you like it.

Videos need to remain fresh and creative. Repetitive, low-effort videos tell a seller exactly the wrong thing. A reel has to hold people from the first second. The longer they watch, the more Instagram shows it, and the further it reaches, including sellers who haven't yet decided who to list with, and even potential buyers.

Each storyboard starts with who the video is for and the feeling it's going for. Two storyboards below (for O Sonho, which has since come off your site; they are just examples):
